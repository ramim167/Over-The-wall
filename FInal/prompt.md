**Submission rule:** the elevator-pitch video must be **3:00 or shorter**. This plan runs **2:55**. Every number below matches the final 15-slide deck (`WaterSmart_Round2_Deck_FINAL15.pptx`).

## 1. Master prompt (paste first, before any scene)

```
Create a premium, cinematic 16:9 brand-pitch film, 2 minutes 55 seconds long, for a business-case
competition judged by Marico Bangladesh. Product: "Parachute Naturale WaterSmart", a shampoo for
Bangladesh's iron-rich and hard water, with a water-hardness test strip inside the 340 ml cap
("PaniCheck") and a QR code to an area water map ("Pani Map"). Team: The Last BrainCell.
Campaign line: "Check your water first."

Story arc: a young "Mover" arrives in a new city → their hair stops feeling right → the real
culprit is the water → the reveal of WaterSmart (formula + test in the cap + Pani Map) → the
gamified #PaniCheckChallenge → why people buy → why it fits Marico → the business case →
a low-risk pilot ask → tagline close.

Look: premium FMCG launch film crossed with an Apple-style product reveal. Deep navy (#141C52)
and Naturale aqua (#12A5C4, #0E7FA0) dominate; warm amber (#F5A31A) is the single accent for
key numbers; rust (#B4511F) only for iron/stain moments. Macro water photography: slow-motion
droplets, light caustics, glass, foam. Shallow depth of field, soft key light, gentle film grain,
smooth motion-controlled camera moves (slow push-ins, orbit around the bottle, top-down macro).
Clean kinetic typography in a geometric sans (Inter / Montserrat style), left-aligned, generous
spacing. No clutter, no cartoon style, no stock-photo smiles.

People: young Bangladeshi adults aged 18 to 30, university hall and mess residents, both women
(some wearing hijab) and men, natural and modest clothing, authentic Dhaka hall/mess
bathrooms with buckets and tube-well or supply-water taps. No celebrities, no real-person
likenesses.

Rules: keep the bottle design identical to the uploaded reference images. Do not render any
readable text, numbers or Bangla letters inside the generated footage; leave clean negative
space where titles will be overlaid in editing. No competitor logos or packs. No medical or
hair-fall claims.
```

## 3. Voice direction (male, professional presenter)

- **Voice:** male, 30–45, polished keynote/brand-film presenter. Warm baritone, crisp diction, clear neutral international English that is easy for Bangladeshi judges to follow.
- **Tone:** confident, calm, premium. Empathetic in scenes 2–4, a lift with a slight smile at the reveal (scene 5), energetic in scene 6, firm and deliberate on numbers (scenes 8–10), warm and proud at the close.
- **Pace:** about 145 words per minute. `/` = short beat (0.3 s), `//` = long beat (0.8 s).
- **Language:** the whole voice-over is in **English**. The Bangla tagline appears only as on-screen text; the voice says its English translation, "Is it your hair, or your water?" and "Check your water first."

## 5. Scene-by-scene shot list

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:06 · Cold open
- **Visual:** black screen → a single water droplet falls in extreme slow motion onto dark hair; it beads, holding a faint rust tint. Pull back to a silhouette under a hall-bathroom tap.
- **On-screen text:** দোষ কি চুলের, নাকি পানির? (large, white) → *Is it your hair, or your water?* (smaller, aqua)
- **VO:** "Is it your hair, // or your water?"
- **SFX:** single drip, low hum.

### Scene 2 · 0:06–0:23 · The Mover
- **Visual:** montage. A young woman with a suitcase arrives at a university hall gate in Dhaka; a young man unpacks in a shared mess room. A bucket fills from an old tap; an **orange stain ring** on the bucket rim (rust accent). She lathers and gets weak, flat foam; she touches her hair, frowning; she pours more shampoo.
- **On-screen text:** New city · New hall · **New water**
- **VO:** "Millions of young Bangladeshis move for study and work. / New city, new hall, / new water. // And slowly, their hair stops feeling like their own: / less lather, / a rough, dull feel, / more shampoo every wash."

### Scene 3 · 0:23–0:42 · The problem is national
- **Visual:** navy background; the district map (`map.png`) draws itself district by district in rust and amber tones. Camera pushes into Dhaka. Then a top-down macro: a strip of tap water ripples over the city.
- **On-screen text (count up):**
  - **58 of 61** districts: most wells iron-rich or hard
  - **65%** of wells above the iron staining level · **58%** hard or very hard
  - **6 of 12** Dhaka tap locations tested hard (2025)
  - Source (small): BGS & DPHE national well survey, 3,530 wells with results, 1998–99 · ICCEI 2025 Dhaka tap study
- **VO:** "It's not in their heads. / In the national well survey, 58 of 61 districts had mostly iron-rich or hard wells. / And in 2025, half the Dhaka tap locations tested were hard. // Water changes from building to building, / so you need to check your own."

### Scene 4 · 0:42–0:55 · The insight
- **Visual:** a real-feeling phone survey UI fades in, then dissolves into 22 small figures; 18 of them light up in amber. Cut to the student in the mirror, two shampoo bottles in her hands, confused.
- **On-screen text:** **18 of 22** who changed water: hair problems got worse · Source (small): Team survey, n=49, 3–9 Oct 2026
- **VO:** "In our survey, 18 of 22 people who changed their water said their hair problems got worse. / They blame their hair. / They blame their shampoo. // Nobody gives them a way to check."

### Scene 5 · 0:55–1:23 · The reveal (hero scene)
- **Visual:**
  1. Music swells. Out of darkness the **340 ml WaterSmart bottle** rises through clear water, aqua light, slow 180° orbit (bottle reference).
  2. **Illustrative animation** (corner tag): calcium, magnesium and iron as rust/grey particles cling to a hair strand and choke the foam bubbles. Then glowing aqua GLDA "claws" lock onto each mineral and rinse them away; the foam rises full and white.
  3. **The cap moment:** macro on the flip-top, *click*, foil peel, the strip slides out. A hand dips it in a bucket for one second; the colour blooms and the **colour-reveal meter** lights up, landing on "Hard" (chime).
  4. A phone scans the QR on a **Tk 3 sachet** → Pani Map UI (`phone.png`) shows the user's district.
- **On-screen text:**
  - Parachute Naturale **WaterSmart** · *The shampoo that checks your water first*
  - **3-in-1:** Chelating formula (GLDA + Coconut Milk Protein) · PaniCheck cap · Pani Map
  - Soft · Moderate · Hard · Very hard
  - Sachet **Tk 3** · 180 ml **Tk 180** · 340 ml + PaniCheck **Tk 360**
- **VO:** "So we created Parachute Naturale WaterSmart: / the shampoo that checks your water first. // A chelating formula with G-L-D-A locks iron, calcium and magnesium so they rinse away, / with Naturale's Coconut Milk Protein. // In every three-forty ml cap, a sealed PaniCheck strip: / dip for one second, / match the colour, / know your water's hardness. // And on every pack, even the 3 taka sachet, / a QR code to Pani Map."

### Scene 6 · 1:23–1:39 · Unique and gamified: #PaniCheckChallenge
- **Visual:** fast, energetic vertical-video frames inside the 16:9 canvas (split screens). Students in different halls dip strips on camera and hold them to the lens; each result triggers the colour-reveal meter and the chime. A share card ("My hall water: HARD · #PaniCheckChallenge", overlaid in editing) flies to a friend, who accepts and tests theirs. Cut to a **campus "Pani Lab" booth**: students queue for a free strip test and a same-shampoo foam demo, then pick up a Tk 3 sachet.
- **On-screen text:** **No shampoo we found offers a water test in the pack** · #PaniCheckChallenge · Test → Share your colour → Challenge a friend · Campus Pani Lab: **30 universities**
- **VO:** "No shampoo we found puts a water test in the pack, / so every bottle becomes a live demo. // With the PaniCheck Challenge, creators and students test their water on camera, / share their colour / and challenge friends to check theirs."

### Scene 7 · 1:39–2:00 · Why people will buy

## 7. Guardrails (tell the tool, and check in edit)

**Must**
- Overlay **all text, numbers and Bangla script in the editor**, never inside AI footage (AI tools garble Bangla letters and digits). Bangla Unicode to paste: `দোষ কি চুলের, নাকি পানির?` and `আগে পানি চেক করুন।`
- Keep the bottle identical to the reference images in every shot.
- Tag every product shot "Concept mock-up" and the mineral/foam sequence "Illustrative animation". The deck says this animation is illustrative, so it must not look like a measured test.
- Put the small source line on every number scene.

**Must not**
- Say or show "stops hair fall", "cures", "clinically proven", "dermatologist approved" or any lab result. The formula claims are proposals for R&D.
- Show competitor logos, packs or brand names (use "premium brands").
- Claim the factory, strip supplier or Marico have approved anything.
- Show real or recognisable people, or children.
- Invent any number not in Section 6.
