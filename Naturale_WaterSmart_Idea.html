1. Case fit

Hair Cleansing ✓

The task's Hair Cleansing category: "Design differentiated hair cleansing solutions for emerging consumer needs and usage occasions." Moving to a hostel or a new city, migrant life and coastal living are all new usage occasions defined by water. CASE p.10

Product + pack ✓

The task asks for "a groundbreaking product idea and/or a unique packaging innovation." WaterSmart is a new formula and the PaniCheck cap. Marico's own case examples are packaging-led: the Root Applicator and the Soft Touch Bottle. CASE p.4–7

3 pivots ✓

Innovation: a water-specific formula plus PaniCheck. Diversification: a Naturale "Water Series" and coastal sachets. Scaling Up: national GT and sachet roll-out after a Dhaka + measured iron/hard-water pilot; later, hard-water markets in Marico Bangladesh's export footprint (e.g. Middle East), subject to each market's registration.

Why we stand apart: the case's biggest example is hair fall, so many teams will pitch oils, serums or applicators for hair fall. We keep a nearby consumer worry (rough, weak, breaking hair) and give it a fresh, testable angle: water. We do not claim water causes hair fall.

2. Insight: three layers, honestly weighted

Consumer belief

"Ever since I moved, my hair feels different."

A belief we expect among hall and mess students, new-city movers, Gulf migrants and coastal families. We ask about hair change before mentioning water, so we don't plant the answer. VALIDATE planned n=25

Science (mixed, limited)

Hard water may weaken hair fibres

Luqman 2018: matched hair segments from 70 men; tensile strength untreated 255.49, deionized 254.84, hard water 234.16 (P=0.001). Luqman 2016: weakening, not hair loss. Srinivasan 2013: no significant difference. All ex vivo; breakage is not hair fall. PMID 30034190 · 27654734 · 24574692

Bangladesh water

Historical and local variation

BGS/DPHE:3,534 wells sampled1998–1999;3,530 valid chemistry records. Team analysis finds Fe median1.1mg/L and Ca/Mg-derived hardness median153.87mg/L asCaCO3. BWDB2019–2021 monitoring and13 selected Dhaka taps add context; neither yields national household exposure or SAM. Explore the dated records.

Existing category abroad; local value remains a question. Dove Hard Water Defense and Malibu C disclose DisodiumEDTA; chelation is not a new mechanism. Imported listings show options, not verified Bangladesh demand or stock. Affordable local alternatives and a performance/value advantage still require pack, shelf and consumer checks.

11. Hostile judge Q&A

"Unilever already has Dove Hard Water Defense. Why can't they just bring it here?"

They can, and Dove's formula already contains a chelator, so chemistry alone is not our moat. Our lead: a saline variant they don't market, the PaniCheck and Pani Map screening and information layer, coconut equity, and GT and sachet reach at Tk 3. Imported hard-water shampoos are already listed online here at premium prices: that shows listed supply options; local demand and affordable shelf alternatives require research.

"Is there real proof that water damages hair?"

The evidence is mixed. Luqman 2018 found lower tensile strength in hair segments treated with hard water; Srinivasan 2013 found no significant difference. Both are lab studies on hair fibres, and neither shows hair loss. So we sell what a lab can measure (mineral build-up removal, lather in qualified test water), never a hair-fall cure. A breakage claim comes only after our own test.

"Can a strip in a cap really tell me about my water?"

Only hardness, and only as a screen. Iron and salinity need separate tests, which we run at activations (iron strip, conductivity meter). The pack says this plainly, and the Pani Map is labelled as a dated regional reference, not a household result.

"Won't a chelating shampoo dry hair if used daily?"

The finished formula is not yet tested. Ingredient choice alone does not prove daily-use mildness; qualified mildness, preservation, sensory and stability studies are mandatory gates.

"Historical model: what if sales are 20% lower?"

Conclusion → number → evidence → action. With volume −20% alone, NPV is (base ). If price −5%, COGS +8% and a 3-month delay hit at the same time, NPV is . Action: hold at the pilot and fix price, COGS or channel mix before going national.

"Does your NPV depend on getting working capital back?"

Partly, so we show both. At the selected assumptions NPV is with the end-of-Y5 working-capital release and without it (IRR ); compare the historical result with the unconfirmed 15% hurdle; the current fiscal case is negative.

"Isn't this just cannibalizing Naturale?"

We show it separately. Standalone EBITDA is the variant's own result; incremental EBITDA subtracts 20% of sales assumed to come from other Naturale variants at 45% contribution. The remainder is an unverified transfer/new-user assumption; Phase 1 excludes LonaJol. No observed switching or paid-repeat data.

"Your research is only 25 people."

n=25 remains planned. Two attributed published samples, 390/391, provide context; five user-confirmed real notes have metadata pending. No team water tests or finished-product trials have been completed/provided. Report actual answered denominators; a qualified pilot is future validation.

"Which regulator?"

DGDA under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 2023 (draft licence and registration rules posted June 2026; final status to confirm), BSTI mandatory certification for shampoo (BDS 1269:2022), and plastic EPR obligations from August 2026. Final claims will be cleared with Marico Regulatory.

"Why is monthly funding higher?"

Full cash schedule uses exit run-rate NWC and actual monthly spend timing. Project M0 is first CAPEX; prelaunch time is included. No static funding number is used.

"What if strips are expensive?"

Obtain a landed quote; target Tk 3–4 under baseline assumptions. Bottle GM alone cannot pass the project. The old return-based ceiling is withdrawn; under the conditional fiscal base, no strip-only cost fix passes. If strips move to activations or launch packs, update costs, redemption/delivery and the pack promise before reappraisal.

"How is repeat measured?"

Paid repurchase in mature, consented SKU cohorts, with receipt verification and lost follow-up reported. Distributor reorder and free incentives are separate. Mandatory product/regulatory/quality gates precede every scale decision.
