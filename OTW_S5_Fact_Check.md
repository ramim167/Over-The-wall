**Verdict: useful competition research, but not fully verified or ready to serve as an unquestioned source of truth.** The case and masterclass summaries largely match the local originals. WaterSmart is a plausible product concept; its proposed performance, novelty, diagnosis system, manufacturing readiness and commercial adoption remain hypotheses. The financial arithmetic reproduces, but the investment appraisal omits an important cash-flow assumption from the written explanation.

This audit covers every section of the master file. “Verified” means either a match to the supplied original or a match to an identified public source. It does not turn a commercial forecast, testimonial or manufacturer's marketing claim into independently established truth. Items that could not be checked are explicitly identified below. No accuracy percentage or probability of winning is assigned.

## 5. WaterSmart science, novelty and product system: Sections 10–11

### Hair and water

The [2018 Luqman study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30034190/) used hair samples from 70 men in Pakistan. The reported control and hard-water tensile-strength values, 255.49 and 234.16, and P=0.001 are correctly reproduced. The experiment concerns treated hair fibres; it is not a trial of people using shampoo and does not demonstrate follicular hair loss. A [2016 Luqman study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654734/) also found reduced strength and explicitly distinguished weakening from hair loss. A [2013 study of 15 volunteers](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24574692/) found no significant strength or elasticity difference under its conditions.

The responsible conclusion is that mineral deposition is a relevant formulation problem and some laboratory studies support weakened fibres under particular conditions. Bangladesh-specific exposure, consumer benefit and the proposed product's effect still require measurement. The categorical tagline “It's not your hair. It's your water” goes beyond this evidence if presented as a diagnosis. Use it as an explicitly framed consumer belief or soften the causal message.

The coastal 29% figure appears in the [FES account](https://asia.fes.de/news/bangladesh-coastal-women.html), which attributes it to a 2019 source. It is a broad coastal-population estimate, not the percentage needing this shampoo. The 73% figure and the hair-fall testimonial appear in the [Mongla report](https://www.tbsnews.net/features/panorama/how-monglas-women-are-bearing-brunt-rising-salinity-1184581). The percentage concerns drinking-water use in five surveyed upazilas; the testimonial is an individual's attribution. Neither proves a national prevalence of water-induced hair loss. The underlying UNDP survey was not retrieved.

### Competition and formulation

[Dove's own page](https://www.dove.com/arabia/en/p/hair-therapy-hard-water-defense-shampoo.html/06281006548497) confirms Mineral Shield positioning, Vitamin E and the oil/shampoo/conditioner system. Its hair-fall claim is qualified as breakage compared with a non-conditioning shampoo. Its ingredients include **Disodium EDTA**. Consequently, “Dove shields; WaterSmart chelates” cannot establish an exclusive ingredient-level difference or superior efficacy without comparative testing.

[Malibu C](https://malibuc.com/products/hard-water-wellness-shampoo) already markets a chelating shampoo for calcium, magnesium and iron buildup. An [indexed Ubuy Bangladesh single-shampoo listing](https://www.ubuy.com.bd/en/product/RVU9L7S-malibu-c-hard-water-wellness-shampoo?Om=om&delete_json=1) describes an imported alternative. Direct retrieval failed during the second review; current stock, checkout price and deliverability are not verified. Other indexed formats include duos and kits. These listings challenge blanket absence claims; they do not disprove a narrowly defined first affordable, locally developed platform. Such a claim still needs a documented audit.

The search-growth percentages match the [February 2026 Beauty Independent/Orbit article](https://orbit.beautyindependent.com/article/hard-water-rush-haircare-brands-retailers-address-mineral-buildup-consumer-awareness-grows), which attributes them to Spate's cross-platform Popularity Index. Keep them as foreign directional trend signals, not Bangladesh demand measurements.

Chelation of hardness minerals, treatment of iron deposits and performance in sodium-chloride-rich water are different formulation requirements. “Coconut-derived” does not itself establish salt tolerance, mildness or mineral removal. Specify the proposed ingredient system, concentration range, pH, water conditions and comparator. All stated launch benefits require formula-specific testing. Existing Naturale products establish a plausible brand and capability fit, not a finished formula or guaranteed manufacturing route.

### PaniCheck and Pani Map

[USGS](https://water.usgs.gov/nwc/NWC/water_quality/tables/quality.abs.html) distinguishes hardness, iron and conductivity; conductivity can approximate dissolved solids but does not identify their composition. A high TDS reading alone is not a hardness or iron result. [Hach's hardness strips](https://www.hach.com/p-hardness-test-strips/2793844) screen principally calcium/magnesium hardness. Separate tests are needed for iron and chloride/salinity. The strip's range and interference limits must fit the sampled water.

Hach sells individually wrapped strips for stability; that is one supplier design, not a universal packaging requirement. The proposed cap compartment needs an appropriate moisture-protection design, instructions, expiry control, compatibility testing and a supplier quotation. A test strip is not automatically just “paper” or an environmental benefit. One strip gives one screening result; water quality can change by season and source.

The master file's BGS link now redirects to a general groundwater page. Use the current [Bangladesh reports and data page](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/groundwater/quality/groundwater-and-health/arsenic-contamination-of-groundwater/bangladesh-reports/). It provides a historical survey of 3,534 boreholes across 61 districts, reported in 2001, with attribution requirements. This cannot establish the present water in every hostel, municipal tap, treated tank or household. The Tangail study link was not successfully retrieved. A [DPHE reference](https://dphe.gov.bd/sites/default/files/files/dphe.portal.gov.bd/policies/b9603e8d_ab66_4d54_aa06_fbafdded1c20/sdpeng.pdf) supports groundwater iron as a concern, but drinking-water acceptability values must not be repurposed as hair-damage thresholds.

### Manufacturing and regulation

The FACTORY checkmarks should be provisional assessments. No production trial, cap/strip BOM, line-capacity confirmation, MOQ, yield data or supplier quotation is supplied. The model implies approximately 231,372 litres in Y1, 585,320 in Y2 and 860,059 in Y3; compare those quantities with an actual capacity commitment before calling the increment modest. “Export-ready” also needs destination-specific evidence. The 4–6-month estimate and nine selling months in Y1 need a consistent calendar covering development, approvals, pilot and rollout.

The [Drugs and Cosmetics Act 2023](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1447.html) establishes cosmetics licensing/registration and transition provisions. A [Health Services Division notice](https://hsd.gov.bd/pages/go-ultimates/%E0%A6%94%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%A7-%E0%A6%AA%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%A8-%E0%A7%A7-%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%96%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%83-%E0%A7%A7%E0%A7%AE%E0%A7%AC-%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B8-%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B8-%E0%A6%93-%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A7%E0%A6%A8-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A7%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE-%E0%A7%A8%E0%A7%A6%E0%A7%A8%E0%A7%AC-8cnhrm-6a3b5fa6ea1dba9cc6991018) verifies publication of draft Cosmetics (Licence and Registration) Rules on **24 June 2026**.
