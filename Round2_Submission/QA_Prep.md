# Judge Q&A prep

Answer in the Finance masterclass order: **conclusion → number → evidence → action**. All numbers are from model v4 (`finance/finance_v4_output.json`).

**1. Does hard water really damage hair?**
The evidence is mixed, so we don't claim damage. Luqman 2018 found lower tensile strength in hard-water-washed hair (234 vs 255, n=70); Srinivasan 2013 found no significant difference (n=15). Our proposed claims are about build-up and lather, which can be measured. R&D tests come before any claim, and we will never say "stops hair fall".

**2. Your water data is from 1998–99. Isn't that outdated?**
Yes, and that's exactly why the product carries a test. BGS/DPHE is the only national well-level dataset (3,534 wells). Newer evidence points the same way: 6 of 12 Dhaka tap locations measured above 120 mg/L in a 2025 study. Pani Map labels the data as area history, and the strip measures today's water.

**3. Why would people pay Tk 360?**
At Tk 360 for 340 ml, WaterSmart costs Tk 106 per 100 ml. Naturale Hijab Fresh sells at Tk 115, Dove at Tk 142 and Clear at Tk 152 (Chaldal, 9 Oct). Our survey measures maximum price before showing Tk 360. The pilot gate checks real rate of sale, and price −5% is in the sensitivity (NPV without recovery falls to −Tk 2.0 Cr).

**4. What if sales are 20% lower?**
NPV without working-capital recovery drops from +Tk 0.5 Cr to −Tk 1.5 Cr. The full worst case (volume −20%, price −5%, COGS +8%, 3-month delay) is −Tk 4.4 Cr. That is why national spend waits for the pilot: only Tk 3.5 Cr is committed before the go/no-go.

**5. Won't this cannibalise Naturale?**
Yes, partly, and the P&L already charges for it: 20% of WaterSmart revenue is assumed to come from Naturale at 45% contribution (Tk 3.7 Cr in Year 3). The target is users of other brands, and the price sits above core Naturale. At 30% cannibalisation, NPV without recovery is −Tk 3.0 Cr, so the pilot measures switching.

**6. Biggest assumption?**
Units per outlet per month. It drives volume and isn't proven. The pilot's first gate is rate of sale at least 70% of plan in ~1,500 outlets.

**7. Is NPV only positive because of working-capital recovery?**
No: it is +Tk 3.1 Cr with recovery and +Tk 0.5 Cr without, IRR 25.1% and 17.0%. Both clear the 15% hurdle, with no terminal value counted. Recovery of working capital at the end of a fixed horizon is standard practice. We show both numbers.

**8. Did you include VAT and supplementary duty?**
Yes. Net realisation is MRP − VAT 15% − trade margin 22% − SD 15%: Tk 360 becomes Tk 212. Gross margin is calculated on net revenue, not MRP. Marico Tax would confirm the exact invoice treatment.

**9. How much cash do you need?**
Peak funding is Tk 9.8 Cr in month 25, the end of the launch year. Of that, Tk 3.5 Cr comes before the go/no-go (CAPEX Tk 3.0 Cr + R&D and pilot Tk 0.5 Cr).

**10. Can a strip survive in a humid bathroom?**
That is a real risk, so it's a gate. Strips are individually foil-sealed (Hach, Bartovation and Macherey-Nagel all offer sealed formats), we use two suppliers, and the strip-complaint gate is below 2%. The 18-month life is a target that supplier stability data must confirm.

**11. Why not just add a chelator to every Naturale variant?**
Because the test and the Pani Map create the reason to choose it. A chelator alone is invisible. The strip turns the bottle into a demo, like Extra Care's root applicator turned packaging into proof.

**12. Isn't Dove already doing this?**
Only in the Gulf, at import prices, and without a test. Dove's pack lists Disodium EDTA. WaterSmart uses GLDA, which is readily biodegradable, is sold in Bangladeshi general trade, offers a Tk 3 sachet, and shows people their problem.

**13. Is the sachet profitable?**
Thinly: about 29% gross margin against 55% on the 340 ml. The sachet's job is reach and trial. The blended margin is 47%, and the pilot gate is at least 45%.

**14. How do you know it's working?**
Leading indicators: water tests done, QR scans, trial. Business indicators: rate of sale, paid repeat in mature cohorts, blended gross margin. The Naturale brand manager owns the P&L, and the decision comes at month 12.
