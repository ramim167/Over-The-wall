## Filming notes
- Shoot horizontal, 1080p, phone on a stand. Record voice separately in a quiet room and lay it over the footage.
- Foam test on camera: same shampoo, same dose (1 ml), same water volume (100 ml), same shaking (10 times), measured at 30 seconds. Film both glasses in one frame with a ruler behind them. Show what actually happened, even if the difference is small.
- Strip shot: use the strip's own colour chart and read time. Say the real number on camera.
- If you add the survey result, say it with its base, for example "[ ] of [ ] people who changed water said their hair got worse."
- Export at 2:55 or less; the limit is 3:00.
