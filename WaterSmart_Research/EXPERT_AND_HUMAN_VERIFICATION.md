# WaterSmart: questions that public research cannot answer

9 October 2026 · Team The Last BrainCell · internal research.

Use this as an evidence request, not a completed checklist. No supplier, respondent, chemist or Marico employee was contacted by the research process. No response has been simulated. Obtain a dated answer/document from the relevant owner, retain the question/version and record refusals/unknowns. An opinion that an approach is plausible is different from a qualified product result.

## B. Marico Tax / Finance — critical before any investment claim

Attach the official 15% local-supply shampoo SD schedule, NBR waterfall example, frozen model and fiscal overlay. Ask:

1. For a locally manufactured HS 3305.10.00 shampoo, what exact invoice/tax base and effective VAT/SD treatment apply to this legal entity and route? Provide a redacted worked invoice and the specific SRO if an exemption is claimed. Does the proposed manufacturer/contract-manufacturer route change the treatment?
2. Are trade percentages measured against consumer MRP, VAT-exclusive invoice, SD-exclusive revenue or another base? Which discounts/rebates/returns are inside that percentage, and are the old 22%/25% assumptions realistic by SKU/channel?
3. How should the FY26 20% tax policy translate into an incremental project forecast? What future rate/conditions, allowable deductions, loss relief, advance taxes, payment dates and minimum tax apply?
4. Does the 5% participation-fund policy allocate to incremental project EBIT? Confirm the correct profit base, tax deductibility, payment timing and treatment of loss months. Avoid adding both an allocated fund cost and the same cost inside overhead.
5. What hurdle/WACC and currency/inflation conventions are approved? Is 15% a company policy or only our planning choice?
6. What inventory, receivable, payable and customer-advance drivers apply by channel/SKU? Provide days/terms and seasonality. Can the project actually release all NWC at year five, or must a continuing-value model be used instead?
7. Which royalty, technical-assistance, shared service, QA, freight, wastage, depreciation, R&D, registration and EPR costs are incremental versus already inside conversion/overhead? What is the allocation rule?
8. Obtain a scoped precommercial cash budget: testing/claims/certification, pilot inventory, recruitment/tracking, tax/NWC, pack tooling and payment milestones. Which omitted costs exceed the existing Tk 0.5cr allowance?
9. Reconcile primary/secondary/tertiary revenue recognition and cannibalization. What evidence supports a 20% transfer from Naturale and 45% displaced contribution? What would be lost from the existing product, rather than total gross sales transferred?

**Evidence acceptance:** dated redacted invoice/SRO, approved project assumptions and complete cash scope. A verbal “tax is included” or a company-wide effective rate is insufficient.

## C. Marico R&D / qualified cosmetic chemist — critical product gate

1. Is the proposed iron/Ca/Mg deposit proposition technically plausible with this actual Naturale base? Which chelators are already present in the current formulation, and what change would be material?
2. What exact grade, active content, supplied-product dosage, pH, surfactant/electrolyte compatibility and impurities must be controlled? Provide formula version, density, batch specification and costing basis; do not assume 1kg/L.
3. Which iron forms/speciation, Ca/Mg ratios, pH and mixed-ion conditions represent the intended water use? What reference methods and standards quantify hair deposits and bath-water chemistry?
4. Design a randomized, blinded matched-tress comparison with current Naturale and an available relevant comparator. Prespecify donor-level replication, wash dose/cycles, mineral endpoints, sensory/combing endpoints, meaningful difference, statistical analysis and sample size. Tresses from one donor are not independent people.
5. Is the proposed ≥30% deposit reduction meaningful and attainable? How should it be expressed, and what uncertainty is needed? If claiming non-inferiority, agree a margin and confidence interval beforehand.
6. Design equal-dose lather/cleansing tests at relevant hardness/iron/mixed-ion ranges. Separate foam height from cleaning/deposition/hair feel. The old ≥80% low-mineral-reference foam gate is proposed, not validated.
7. What safety, irritation/sensitization, preservative challenge, microbiological, compatibility and stability studies are required for the actual formula and pack? Who can release a batch for adult home use?
8. What real-time/accelerated evidence supports the **finished product** and **sealed strip** expiry in Bangladesh storage? What happens after foil damage or a wet cap?
9. What truthful pack/advertising claims are supported by the finished data, and what medical/causal claims must be removed? Does “helps remove mineral build-up” match the tested minerals and comparator?
10. LonaJol separately: define NaCl/mixed-ion conditions, formulation compatibility, mildness/preservation/stability and coastal product-use study before any saline claim or rollout.

**Evidence acceptance:** dated written approach review followed by versioned protocols and actual test reports. Published ingredient brochures cannot pass the finished-product gate.

## D. Strip supplier RFQ — copyable request, not sent

> We are evaluating an individually moisture-sealed, total-hardness water-screen strip for a proposed shampoo launch pack in Bangladesh. The strip is for consumer screening, not drinking-water safety or medical diagnosis. Please quote your exact product/grade and provide: test units/range/colour blocks/read time; strip and foil dimensions/tolerances; minimum order by quantity tier; unit cost including custom chart/foil and printing/tooling; currency, incoterm, freight, import classification, payment terms and lead time; expiry from manufacture and supported storage/humidity conditions; Fe/Cu/salinity/pH interferences; CoA and accuracy/agreement data against a reference method; foil-barrier and seal data; batch traceability and replacement terms; and sample availability. Identify which evidence supports an 18-month usable life in relevant Bangladesh storage, or state a shorter supported life. Please separate catalogue, sample and volume-production pricing.

Ask the packaging engineer before accepting a quote: will the stated dimensions fit the cap without folding/damaging the reagent or compromising closure? Does price include a chart, sleeve, insertion, wastage, landed duties and shipping? Are any freight/duty elements already in the old COGS allocation? Does the minimum shelf life remaining on arrival match product logistics?

The known MN grade has one-year life at 4–30°C. Do not claim that a three-month accelerated screen or a Tk 3 target answers the RFQ. Hach price-on-request and catalogue examples are not quotations.

## E. Marico Packaging / Manufacturing / Procurement — critical operational gate

1. Confirm exact bottle neck/closure dimensions, cap pocket geometry, seal/torque, dispensing, drop/leak and tamper protection. Can shampoo or bathroom water contact the strip?
2. Provide measured clean/dry BOM mass for bottle, cap, label, foil/sleeve, strip substrate/reagent, shipper and sachet laminate. Separate plastic by material/category from total composite mass. Retain measurement method, repeats and sample batch.
3. Quote full bottle/cap/mould/foil/insertion, formula materials, conversion/QC, energy, yield/rejection/wastage, shipper and freight at realistic volume tiers. Are taxes and recoverable credits treated correctly?
4. Show actual bulk/filling available hours, line speed and changeover capacity; how much is reserved for existing Naturale? FY26 improvements do not answer incremental available capacity.
5. Can current FFS fill the proposed 6ml formulation accurately? What density/viscosity and tolerances apply? Does sachet laminate/printing comply with current SUP/EPR requirements?
6. Is manual pilot insertion safe/controlled, and what equipment/environment/checks are required nationally? Obtain OEE, strip damage/missing rates and vision/traceability controls from an actual trial.
7. Provide CAPEX quotation, commissioning dates and installation/certification/changeover downtime. What scope is outside Tk 3cr?

**Evidence acceptance:** dimensioned drawings, physical samples, measured BOM, versioned quotations and controlled line trials; a render is not a qualified pack.

## F. Regulatory / ESG — mandatory before pilot sale

1. Obtain BDS 1269:2022 and exact applicable tests/limits, claims/labelling and certification route for a new Naturale variant. Check pH against the actual standard, without inferring numerical limits from an inaccessible preview.
2. Confirm operative DGDA cosmetics rules/orders, transition, entity/product licences/registration and evidence requirements as of the proposed launch date. Identify the final gazette if available; a draft is not operative law.
3. Confirm any sachet/SUP restrictions applicable to this pack and timing. EPR obligations and product-pack permissibility are distinct questions.
4. What is Marico's actual EPR listing/registration date and category assignment? How are rigid bottles, flexible/multilayer sachets and strip-cap/foil accessories classified?
5. Establish category-specific placed-on-market quantities and collection/recycling obligations, approved recycler/PRO route, municipal exclusions and credit eligibility. Quote logistics, collection, recycling, admin/reporting and verification costs.
6. What evidence must be retained for annual reporting and pack claims? The model's 1% NR provision cannot substitute for tonnage targets or compliance records.

## G. Consumer Insights / Sales — demand cannot be supplied by a generic survey

For exploratory interviews use the preserved neutral instrument in `WaterSmart_Field_Data_Plan.html`, retaining versions and actual answered denominators:

1. Ask recent hair changes and unprompted causes **before mentioning water**. Ask moves/source changes and timing separately; do not assume every rough-hair complaint came from water.
2. Record current shampoo/pack/actual paid price, water source and treatment, current workarounds/spend. Water beliefs are separate from laboratory measurement.
3. Show an accurate proposed concept with **hardness-only** limits and unvalidated benefits. Include a strip-free or activation-based alternative fairly; do not promise an unavailable cost/shelf life.
4. Ask open maximum WTP **before** Tk 330. Then ask response to the stated price; rotate comparison order, include “none”, record comprehension/reasons. Ask sachet trial separately from bottle preference.
5. Compare actual Movers and a suitable non-mover group rather than assuming the segment wins. Prespecify the age/current-residence definitions; do not extrapolate five urban/premium-user notes to rural shoppers.
6. During a qualified commercial pilot collect deduplicated SKU/outlet sell-out, availability, actual price/returns, loading/reorders and comparable baseline/control outlets. DMS alone may measure distributor shipments rather than consumer demand.
7. Recruit consented buyer cohorts, verify paid first/repeat transactions, use mature SKU windows, and report loss-to-follow-up. A 340ml 180-day cohort may need follow-up beyond a six-month pilot.
8. Validate actual cannibalization and alternatives. Survey intent, retailer reorder, free gifts, QR incentives and global reviews cannot be recoded as paid repeat.

Sample size must follow the endpoint, expected variation, effect/precision and recruitment design. The old n=25/n≈100 plans are not statistically justified national prevalence samples. Do not invent “near-real” respondent rows from a few answers.
