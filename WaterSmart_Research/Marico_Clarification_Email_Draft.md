**Subject:** OTW S5 Round 2 — Clarification on Required Evidence & Financial Detail | The Last BrainCell

Dear OTW Organizing Team,

We are Team **The Last BrainCell**, preparing our Round 2 business proposal in the **Hair Cleansing** category.

With the **10 October deadline** approaching, we would appreciate clarification on the expected level of detail and evidence. For each topic below, could you first confirm whether the specified detail is required for Round 2? If yes, please clarify the minimum acceptable scope or suggest suitable references.

1. **Tax and price waterfall**  
   Is a detailed MRP-to-net-revenue calculation, including VAT, supplementary duty and trade discounts, required?  
   **If yes,** which applicable rates and calculation bases should a locally manufactured shampoo proposal use? Could you provide a simple example or public reference, including any relevant exemption conditions?

2. **Costing and investment**  
   Are itemized COGS, packaging, CAPEX, R&D/testing, certification and pilot-cost estimates required?  
   **If yes,** what level of breakdown is expected? Are supplier quotations necessary, or are credible secondary benchmarks with clearly disclosed assumptions acceptable? Any non-confidential cost references would be helpful.

3. **Technical feasibility and validation**  
   Is formulation/manufacturing feasibility evidence beyond a conceptual explanation required at this stage? Is a physical prototype or actual laboratory testing mandatory?  
   **If yes,** what minimum evidence is acceptable: published research, a qualified expert review, a prototype or test results? Which safety, performance, stability, shelf-life and packaging aspects must be demonstrated now, and which may be presented as future validation gates?

4. **Financial appraisal**  
   Are cash-flow projections, NPV/IRR, peak funding, working capital, corporate tax and participation-fund allocations required?  
   **If yes,** what forecast horizon, appraisal metrics and level of detail are expected? Is there a recommended hurdle rate or benchmark for tax/fund treatment, working-capital terms, A&P, selling/distribution and other incremental costs?

5. **Channel and sales projections**  
   Are channel-wise outlet coverage, sales per outlet, distributor/retailer margins, launch ramp and cannibalization estimates required?  
   **If yes,** what detail is expected, and are non-confidential benchmarks or recommended sources available? Should forecasts be expressed as distributor shipments, retailer purchases or consumer sell-out?

6. **Regulatory and sustainability detail**  
   Is a detailed BSTI/DGDA compliance roadmap, claim-substantiation plan and plastic/EPR assessment required in the proposal?  
   **If yes,** which permissions, tests, packaging restrictions, waste classifications and compliance-cost elements must be addressed at Round 2?

7. **Primary research**  
   Is primary consumer research mandatory, and is there any minimum sample or method requirement?  
   **If yes,** what scope and documentation are expected? Can a small, documented exploratory sample be combined with attributed published studies, with its limitations stated?

8. **Presentation requirements**  
   Are there mandatory content sections or additional presentation rules beyond the supplied case/template?  
   **If yes,** please identify them and confirm the maximum total slide count, whether cover/appendix count within that limit, and the pitch-video duration limit.

A brief pointwise response distinguishing **required**, **recommended** and **optional** detail would help us prioritize appropriately. We will clearly identify sources, assumptions and future validation work. Non-confidential guidance or relevant references would be sufficient.
