APPENDIX B
Evidence base, method and sensitivity
Water: 
BGS & DPHE (2001) National Hydrochemical Survey: 3,534 wells, 61 districts, sampled Mar 1998–Dec 1999. Team analysis: hardness = 2.497 Ca + 4.118 Mg (USGS); flags Fe >0.3 and hardness >120 mg/L. Historical wells, not today’s taps, which is why the pack carries a test.
Recent water: 
ICCEI 2025 Dhaka tap study (12 locations with values); BWDB 2022 groundwater-quality report (901 monitoring wells, 2019–21).
Consumers: 
Khanam & Rahman 2022 (n=390); Zakaria et al. 2025 (n=391). Our own survey and foam test run before the pilot.
Hair science: 
Luqman 2018 (n=70: tensile strength 255 vs 234 with hard water); Srinivasan 2013 (n=15: no significant difference). Evidence is mixed, so claims wait for our own R&D tests.
Market and prices: 
Mordor Intelligence (2026e); 24 online price observations, Chaldal and Arogga, 9 Oct 2026; Marico Bangladesh Annual Report FY2025-26.
R&D TEST PLAN   
Hair-tress deposit test: at least 30% less Ca/Fe than Naturale core. Lather at least 80% of a soft-water reference at 150 and 300 mg/L. Home-use test n≈100. Stability at 40°C / 75% RH.
Biggest levers: cannibalisation and COGS. Both are measured in the pilot before any national spend.
Hair-science references: Luqman et al. 2018 (PMID 30034190); Srinivasan et al. 2013 (PMID 24574692). Sensitivities from finance_v4.cjs.
