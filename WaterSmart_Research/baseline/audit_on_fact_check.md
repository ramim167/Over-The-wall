# Fact-Check: Auditor Claims on the Parachute Naturale WaterSmart Research File (Marico Bangladesh "Over The Wall" Season 5)

Most of the auditor's claims check out, but several need correcting before the file is submitted: the Malibu C product shows up on Ubuy Bangladesh only as a shampoo-and-conditioner duo, the DGDA cosmetics rules exist only as a June 2026 draft, and nobody has reported that the December 2025 "Global Grand Finale" took place.

## TL;DR
- **Confirmed true:** the Dove Hard Water Defense INCI list contains Disodium EDTA; shampoo is under BSTI mandatory certification as BDS 1269:2022; Marico's fourth "D" is Diversity; the OTW Season 2 second runner-up was "Mind Crusaders" (a combined BUP + IUB team); BGS/DPHE sampled 3,534 boreholes in 61 of 64 districts; the 2024 polythene enforcement dates are correct; the hard-water hair studies are described correctly, including the 2018 Luqman figures (255.49 untreated baseline vs 234.16 hard water, P=0.001, per the PMC abstract of PMID 30034190).
- **Needs correcting:** the "single Malibu C shampoo on Ubuy BD" wording (what is listed is a duo, with no price shown); any statement that cosmetics rules under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 2023 are in force (only a draft dated 24 June 2026 was found); any statement that shampoo sachets are banned (they are not; they fall under the 2026 EPR Guidelines instead).
- **Can't be verified:** results of the December 2025 Global Grand Finale; the share of BGS wells above 1 mg/L iron (only the median of 1.1 mg/L could be sourced); any Bangladeshi local brand selling a hard-water shampoo (none found, which actually supports WaterSmart's whitespace claim).

## Notes by Claim

**1. Hard-water shampoos in Bangladesh.**
- **What is listed on Ubuy BD:** Malibu C appears as a Shampoo + Conditioner 33.8 oz duo (Item #53794241).\[3\] I found no single-shampoo Malibu C listing on ubuy.com.bd. The single 9 oz shampoo is on Ubuy India, which lists Bangladesh as a delivery country.\[22\] Ubuy BD also lists ion Hard Water Shampoo and OUAI Detox Shampoo.\[1\]\[2\] None of these pages shows a BDT price (the price field reads "KWD --").
- **Other Bangladeshi retailers:**
  - Daraz.com.bd and Shajgoj: no hard-water shampoo listings found. The Dove Hard Water Defense tag page exists only on Daraz Pakistan.\[23\]
  - CutPriceBD: the only BDT hard-water listing found, "Pantene Pro V hard Water Shampoo", from BDT 550 (a third-party importer).\[24\]
  - Arogga, DearMe and GroomlyBD: Bare Anatomy is sold, but only non-hard-water lines, at roughly ৳1,295–1,650 for 250 ml.\[25\]\[26\]\[27\]
- **Local brands:** I found no Unilever Bangladesh, Square, Kohinoor, ACI or Marico shampoo marketed for hard water. Unilever's locally made Dove variant is "Environmental Defense" (Chaldal, "Product of Bangladesh", 340 ml, ৳370), which is positioned against pollution, not minerals.\[28\]
- **What this means for the file:** imported hard-water shampoos exist only as cross-border premium listings. That supports the whitespace argument, but the file should say "a duo listing on Ubuy BD, no BDT price shown."

**2. Dove Disodium EDTA.** The official Dove Arabia product page confirms it.\[4\] Disodium EDTA is a standard chelator in most Dove shampoos, including the US Hydration Spa and Scalp + Hair Therapy lines.\[29\]\[30\] So EDTA alone does not set the Hard Water Defense product apart; the marketed difference is Dove's "Mineral Shield Technology."\[4\]

**3. Hard-water hair science.**
- **The three studies:**
  - **(a)** PMID 24574692: hard water at 212.5 ppm CaCO3, tensile strength 105.28 vs 103.66, P=0.858.\[5\]
  - **(b)** PMID 27654734: hair from 76 men from Peshawar, treated with Kohat hard water for 10 minutes on alternate days for 3 months.\[6\]
  - **(c)** PMID 30034190: the PMC abstract reports mean tensile strengths of "255.49, 254.84, and 234.16" for Groups A (untreated baseline), B (deionised water) and C (hard water), with hard water significant at "P = 0.001" versus deionised water at "P = 0.609". The auditor's 255.49 vs 234.16, P=0.001 is therefore correct, but 255.49 should be labelled as the untreated baseline, not the deionised-water control (254.84).
- **What the evidence supports:** all three are small ex vivo studies on cut hair strands.\[31\] Claims should be limited to "may reduce hair-shaft tensile strength." Do not claim the product prevents hair fall.

**4. BSTI.**
- **Current list:** BSTI's 315-product list (December 2024 upload) shows shampoo under BDS 1269:2022, 4th revision.\[8\] Older lists (2018 and the 227-item list) cite BDS 1269:2014.\[32\]\[33\]
- **Possible regulatory overlap:** the 2023 Act moved cosmetics licensing to DGDA.\[34\] A product could therefore face both a BSTI CM licence and DGDA registration while the transition is incomplete.

**5. DGDA cosmetics rules.**
- **Status:** the Act was passed and gazetted on 18 September 2023.\[9\] Section 32 requires cosmetics registration, with details "to be prescribed by rules."\[35\] Existing businesses must apply within six months of the rules or orders being issued.\[36\]
- **Latest step found:** a draft "Cosmetics (Licence and Registration) Rules 2026" posted for public comment on 24 June 2026.\[10\] I found no final gazette notification as of 9 October 2026.
- **How to phrase it in the file:** rules pending; registration obligation imminent.

**6. 4D.** Diversity is confirmed in the FY25 report and in the June 2025 and March 2026 investor presentations.\[11\]\[37\]\[38\] The March 2026 deck names the "Parachute Naturale Shampoo range" in Bangladesh as an international diversification example, which is useful for strategic fit.\[38\]

**7. OTW Season 2.**
- Champion Team Payback (BUP), first runner-up "Fast" (IBA, DU) and second runner-up "Mind Crusaders" (BUP + IUB) are all confirmed on Marico's OTW site (5 November 2023).\[12\]
- Correct the auditor on one point: Mind Crusaders was a combined two-university team, not a single-university team.

**8. Marico Bangladesh metrics.**
- **The figures:** "1.1 million+ outlets," "4 out of 5," 99.9% local manufacturing and the export list all come from Marico's own undated web pages.\[13\]
- **The phrasing differs between pages:** "4 out of 5 Bangladeshis" on one page, "4 out of every 5 households" on the current About page.\[13\]\[14\]
- **Earlier figures:** the 2020 annual report said 99% locally made and 4 of 5 households.\[39\] The 2021 annual report said 87% household reach and ~1.4 million outlets.\[40\]
- **Conflicting figures:** Business Standard (India), 13 August 2024, says "Marico has around 77,000 outlets in Bangladesh", likely direct coverage, while Daily Star FY25 results stories cite "over 790,000 outlets" from Marico's website. Cite Marico's web page with an access date and avoid mixing the metrics.

**9. Plastics.**
- **Laws and enforcement:**
  - The 2002 ban rests on Section 6A of the Environment Conservation Act 1995 (as amended in 2002).\[41\]
  - Superstore enforcement began on 1 October 2024, following the adviser's 9 September directive.\[42\]\[43\]
  - Kitchen-market enforcement began on 1 November 2024. One outlet describes this phase as initially 10 Dhaka markets.\[44\]
- **The 17-item list:**
  - The circular date is disputed. FE reported a "recent circular" on 11 July 2024 under the Solid Waste Management Rules 2021, while Mongabay cites 28 August 2024 and the Daily Star and UNB say "August 2024."\[15\]\[41\]\[42\]\[45\]\[46\] It was most likely issued in July and re-circulated in August.
  - Items include cigarette filters, plastic bottles and caps, straws and stirrers, cutlery, Styrofoam food packs, banners under 100 microns, invitation cards, thin films, melamine sponges, sachets and pouches.\[15\]\[42\]
  - The list is a phase-out target, not an enforced ban.\[41\]
- **Shampoo sachets:** no rule bans them. The EPR Guidelines 2026 (S.R.O. No. 293-Law/2026, gazetted 13 August 2026) classify "shampoo sachets" as flexible plastic. According to The Business Standard (20 August 2026, citing a ministry press release), producers must collect "at least 15% of waste and recycl[e] 7.5% in the first two years, and collect 30% and recycle 15% in subsequent years"; The Financial Express (22 August 2026) adds that targets apply "separately for each plastic category."
- **What this means for the file:** WaterSmart's pack plan must budget for EPR obligations.

**10. BGS/DPHE NHS.**
- **Confirmed:** 3,534 boreholes in 61 of 64 districts and 433 upazilas. The work was Phase 2 (1998–2001), published as BGS Technical Report WC/00/19 (2001).\[17\]\[47\]
- **Iron:** the median of 1.1 mg/L (IQR 0.15–4.57, N=3,530) is cited from the BGS data by Naser et al. (icddr,b/Emory), "Effect of Groundwater Iron on Residual Chlorine…", Am J Trop Med Hyg 2018;98(4):977–983 (PMID 29436334); that study's own 654 wells in Gazipur, Mymensingh and Tangail had a median of "0.91 (IQR: 0.36–2.01) mg/L." Because the median sits just above 1 mg/L, roughly half the wells would exceed 1 mg/L. That is an inference; I did not find an exact sourced percentage.
- **Working URL:** the bgs.ac.uk/groundwater page listed in the table (modified 28 November 2025).\[17\] The legacy www2.bgs.ac.uk page also still appears in search results.\[48\]

**11. Testing.**
- **Hardness strips:** they use calmagite and EDTA chemistry. They report total hardness (mainly Ca and Mg), but iron and copper can interfere, so "only Ca/Mg" is slightly overstated.\[19\]
- **TDS meters:** they estimate dissolved solids from conductivity and cannot tell which ions are present, so they cannot identify iron or hardness.\[20\]\[49\]
- **Sourcing gap:** I could not retrieve a USGS page for this. The TDS evidence comes from manufacturer and vendor documentation (Sensorex), so the sourcing is adequate but not ideal.

**12. Global Grand Finale.** The Daily Star, TBS (last modified 8 December 2025) and The Financial Express (26 November 2025, naming Champion "Team Jinxed" (BUP), First Runner-up "Team Oops We Tried" (IUT) and Second Runner-up "Team The Lannisters" (University of Dhaka), from more than 6,000 registrations across over 45 campuses) all say only that the teams "will represent Bangladesh." No source reports that the event took place or gives results. Marico's OTW news page stops at Season 3.\[12\] Do not claim participation or results.

## Recommendations
- Keep claim 3c's figures, but label 255.49 as the untreated baseline (deionised-water group 254.84, per the PMC abstract), and cite the PMIDs (24574692, 27654734, 30034190). Present the evidence as mixed, ex vivo, and about hair-strength only.
- Reword claim 1 to describe Ubuy BD's duo listing, with no price shown, and add "no local brand found." That strengthens the whitespace argument.
- Reword claim 5 as "draft rules published 24 June 2026; final rules pending." Reword claim 9 as "no sachet ban; EPR Guidelines 2026 apply."
- Remove or soften claim 12 unless the team can get confirmation from Marico.

## Caveats
- E-commerce listings change daily, and Ubuy pages are cross-border aggregator listings that may not mean stock is held locally.
- Marico's metrics come from undated corporate marketing copy.
- The share of wells above 1 mg/L iron is an inference from the median reported by Naser et al. (2018) and should be checked against the BGS full dataset.
