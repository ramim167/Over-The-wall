FAQs
How often should you use Malibu C Hard Water Wellness Shampoo?
Unlike other hard water shampoos that may strip hair of natural oils, Malibu C Hard Water Wellness Shampoo can be used daily without stripping hair of natural moisture or as often as needed.
Where can you buy Malibu C Hard Water Wellness Shampoo?
Malibu C Hard Water Wellness Shampoo can be purchased at local salons, MalibuC.com, Amazon, and CVS.
Does hard water cause hair loss?
Hard water may contribute to hair loss. Hard water contains high levels of minerals like calcium, magnesium, and others that can build up on the hair and scalp, potentially blocking pores and affecting scalp health.
What does hard water do to your hair?
Hard water deposits minerals such as calcium and magnesium that can cause hair to feel dry, brittle, and may even cause discoloration. This mineral buildup can also negatively impact scalp health and can lead to a dry, itchy-feeling scalp.
Does Hard Water Wellness Shampoo work with hair damaged by well water?
Yes! Due to well water being drawn directly from the earth, it often is full of minerals that may be damaging to your hair & scalp. Our Malibu C Hard Water Wellness Shampoo helps remove these hard minerals from your hair, helping your hair and scalp feel light and refreshed.
