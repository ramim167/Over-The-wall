Marico Bangladesh Limited has forged a growth journey of 26 years, winning the hearts and minds of millions of households across Bangladesh. Today, the Company stands among the country's top three FMCG companies, recognized for its consistent growth and diversified portfolio across Haircare, Skincare, Baby Care, Male Grooming, and Foods. Through a strong distribution network, Marico Bangladesh reaches 4 out of every 5 Bangladeshi households with trusted brands including Parachute Advansed, Just For Baby, Nihar, Parachute SkinPure, Parachute Naturale, Saffola Active, HairCode, and Livon. Our flagship brand, Parachute Advansed, remains a cherished household name and continues to hold a leading position in the Hair Oil category.

Since its inception in 1999, Marico Bangladesh has operated as a subsidiary of Marico Limited, a multinational FMCG organization selling its products in 50+ countries. Reinforcing our commitment to 'Made in Bangladesh,' 99.9% of our portfolio is manufactured locally, while its products are exported to India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, the Middle East, Egypt, and Vietnam, showcasing the global competitiveness of brands proudly made in Bangladesh.
