Competitive Landscape
Leading Manufacturers Consolidate Share Amid a Highly Concentrated Market
The hair care market in Bangladesh exhibited a highly concentrated structure in 2025, with the top two manufacturers, Unilever Bangladesh Ltd and Marico Ltd, holding a combined retail value share of 85%. Unilever led with a 51% share, remaining stable since 2020, while Marico followed with 34%, increasing from 33% in 2020.
Preference for Natural Formulations Supports Brand Loyalty and Growth Opportunities
Consumer preference increasingly favours natural and nourishing hair care products, influencing competitive dynamics across the category. Marico’s Parachute brand capitalises on this shift, with strong consumer feedback driven by its perceived natural positioning, supporting high repeat purchase rates and brand loyalty.
