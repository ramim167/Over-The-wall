About Marico Bangladesh Limited
Marico Bangladesh Limited is a fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) company operating across personal care, beauty, wellness and food categories in Bangladesh. The company was incorporated in 1999 as a subsidiary of India-based Marico Limited.
According to the company information provided for this page, Marico Bangladesh reaches around four out of every five households in the country and manufactures about 99.9% of its portfolio locally. Its manufacturing facilities are located at Shirirchala and Mouchak in Kaliakoir, Gazipur, while its corporate headquarters is at The Glass House in Gulshan, Dhaka.
Marico Bangladesh is listed on both the Dhaka Stock Exchange and Chittagong Stock Exchange under the ticker
MARICO
. Its portfolio in Bangladesh covers hair care, skin and body care, baby care, food, edible oil and male grooming products, with brands such as Parachute, Saffola, Livon, Just for Baby and others.
