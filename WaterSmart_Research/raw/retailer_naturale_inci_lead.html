HAIR CARE BUNDLE - Parachute Naturale Shampoo Egg Shine 320ml & Onion Enriched Coconut Hair Growth Oil 200ml

The Parachute Naturale Egg Shine Shampoo is enriched with the natural goodness of Coconut Milk and Egg Protein. This shampoo nourishes your hair from roots and helps retain moisture, giving you shiny-strong hair with a pleasant smell. It is widely available in Bangladesh.  Coconut Milk Protein is known to nourish hair from roots, making it strong while Egg Protein is known to give your hair a flawless shine. It fights dryness, relieves irritation, calms itchiness, and reduces redness and inflammation. This shampoo is free from parabens. It leaves your hair strong, shiny, and silky. 

Key Benefits of the Parachute Naturale Shampoo Egg Shine

  • Enriched with Egg protein and Coconut Milk.  
  • Fights dryness and relieves irritation.

  • Prevents scalp inflammation.
  • Controls and prevents greasiness on the scalp.
  • Clinically proven and tested by dermatologists.
  • Comes with a fresh fragrance.

  • Makes your hair shiny and healthy. 
  • Free from parabens.

How to Use

  • Wet your hair with clean water.
  • Shake before use. 
  • Take the necessary amount onto your palms.
  • Apply slightly to your hair.
  • Try to lather it well on your hair.
  • Gently massage onto the scalp.
  • Massage carefully with your fingertips.
  • Make a lather. 
  • Wash off with clean water when done.
  • Repeat if desired.

Disclaimer

  • For external use only.
  • Keep it far away from children.
  • Avoid contact with sensitive areas.
  • If contact with eyes or lips rinse immediately.

Storage

  • Store in a dry and cool place.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight.
