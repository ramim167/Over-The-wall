Problem & platform

Water chemistry varies. Published Bangladesh samples report relevant concerns. Marico has a real Naturale shampoo platform and documented FY26 batch/filling improvements.

Strong category/platform fit. Product performance, the cap design and target-segment advantage remain qualification questions.

Critical design gap

649 historical wells had Fe >1mg/L while calculated hardness was ≤120mg/L. A hardness-only strip cannot rule out iron.

Separate iron testing and truthful hardness-only instructions are necessary. Hardness is not salinity or water safety.

Team analysis of BGS observations · n=3,530 valid wells · Reproduced results

Cap sourcing

Sealed and custom hardness-strip offerings exist. No landed OEM quote or cap trial was obtained. An identified MN grade supports one-year life at4–30°C, shorter than the proposed18months.

Obtain actual cost/dimensions and a supported expiry before promising a launch cap.
