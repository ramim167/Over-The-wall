## Important corrections and supporting evidence

### 1. The Malibu C correction should not be accepted as written

The [indexed Ubuy Bangladesh single-shampoo page](https://www.ubuy.com.bd/en/product/RVU9L7S-malibu-c-hard-water-wellness-shampoo?Om=om&delete_json=1) describes a 3.4 oz Malibu C hard-water shampoo and displays **BDT 2,182**, excluding shipping/customs. Other indexed listings include a duo and a kit. The single page's direct retrieval failed during this review, and the indexed delivery date is stale. Thus current stock, checkout price and deliverability remain unverified.

Use: “Bangladesh-facing import listings include single-shampoo, duo and kit formats; local stock and current landed prices have not been verified.” A product listing cannot prove a domestic mass-market launch. Equally, the audit's unsuccessful search cannot establish that a single-shampoo listing does not exist.

The original first-in-country language remains unsupported. An affordable local water-focused platform could still be a plausible opportunity; that narrower claim needs a documented competitor/retail audit. The phrase “only cross-border premium listings” should also remain qualified, particularly because the supplied audit itself names a Bangladeshi importer listing.

### 2. The cosmetics draft is real; “final rules absent” remains unconfirmed

The [Health Services Division notice](https://hsd.gov.bd/pages/go-ultimates/%E0%A6%94%E0%A6%B7%E0%A6%A7-%E0%A6%AA%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%A8-%E0%A7%A7-%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%96%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%83-%E0%A7%A7%E0%A7%AE%E0%A7%AC-%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%B8-%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B8-%E0%A6%93-%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%A8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A7%E0%A6%A8-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A7%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%AE%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE-%E0%A7%A8%E0%A7%A6%E0%A7%A8%E0%A7%AC-8cnhrm-6a3b5fa6ea1dba9cc6991018) is dated **24 June 2026** and requests comments on draft Cosmetics (Licence and Registration) Rules 2026. Its [72-page attachment](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-hsd/2026/5/561c0ca9-3940-4314-95d9-a92817d758c1.pdf) has blank notification date/SRO fields on page 1, consistent with a draft.

The [2023 Act](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1447.html) separately establishes licensing, registration, orders and transition provisions. Replace “rules pending; registration obligation imminent” with: “A June 2026 draft is verified; the latest final rules/orders and the applicable transition timetable require confirmation.” Do not present draft provisions or fees as operative requirements.

### 3. EPR is a substantive addition, with implementation conditions

The [environment ministry's 20 August 2026 release](https://moef.gov.bd/pages/press-briefings/%E0%A6%AA%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B2%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%9F%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%9C%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AF-%E0%A6%AC%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AF%E0%A6%AC%E0%A6%B8%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A5%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A7%9F-%E0%A6%89%E0%A7%8E%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%80%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%AE%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AA%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%B8%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A4-%E0%A6%A6%E0%A6%BE%E0%A7%9F%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%A4%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%AC-%E0%A6%87%E0%A6%AA%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%86%E0%A6%B0-%E0%A6%A8%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%B0%E0%A7%8D%E0%A6%A6%E0%A7%87%E0%A6%B6%E0%A6%BF%E0%A6%95%E0%A6%BE-%E0%A6%9C%E0%A6%BE%E0%A6%B0%E0%A6%BF-w02fso-6a8a75ac180aff5b553729ca) confirms that the 2026 plastic EPR guidelines were gazetted/enforced on **13 August 2026**. It states phased entity listing and initial collection/recycling targets of **15%/7.5%**, increasing to **30%/15%**.

Registration is required within six months of listing. The SRO number, category details and target bases need the original Gazette, which was not retrieved. Add direct EPR citations. EPR coverage does not establish absence of a sachet prohibition.

### 4. Corporate footprint and team affiliations can be upgraded

[Marico Bangladesh's official page](https://marico.com/bangladesh/about-us) displays **1.1 Mn approximate distribution footprint**. It also displays 90% household reach while narrative text says four of five households. Cite each measure with its wording and access date; do not reconcile differing marketing figures without an explanation. Do not convert approximately 1.1 million into “more than 1.1 million.”

The [indexed official Season 2 announcement](https://overthewall.maricocareers.com/news?page=2), dated 5 November 2023, identifies Mind Crusaders as a combined BUP/Independent University team. This resolves an earlier unverified detail, rather than correcting a claim that the team belonged to just one university.

### 5. Update the BSTI list count

The [official BSTI directory](https://bsti.gov.bd/site/page/741c4948-53e2-455e-bb38-f4281381f426/List-of-Mandatory-Products-) lists **328 mandatory products**, including the 315-item list published 3 June 2025 plus 13 additions dated 29 January 2026. The older list remains valid evidence for shampoo's BDS 1269:2022 entry; its URL's upload directory is not proof of the list's publication date.

### 6. Correct the iron inference and avoid a health-threshold extrapolation

[Naser et al.'s PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29436334/) verifies 654 sampled tube wells and a median iron concentration of **0.91 mg/L**, IQR **0.36–2.01**. The BGS survey count is independently confirmed by [BGS](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/groundwater/quality/groundwater-and-health/arsenic-contamination-of-groundwater/bangladesh-reports/). The national median/IQR and 433-upazila detail require the full paper/report or dataset; they were not independently recovered in this review.

If a dataset's median is above 1 mg/L, **at least half** its observations must be above that threshold. It does not establish that **roughly half** are above it; the proportion can be considerably greater. Compute the actual proportion from valid measured records if needed. Do not extrapolate a historical well distribution to the national population, households needing shampoo or a hair-damage threshold.

### 7. Several “corrections” were already accounted for

The supplied audit critiques claims the earlier fact check did not make:

- It did not say final cosmetics rules were in force.
- It did not say Mind Crusaders was a single-university team.
- It did not say hardness strips respond only to Ca/Mg; it used “principally” and mentioned interference limits.
- It did not say shampoo sachets were banned.
- It did not say the December global finale occurred; it explicitly called participation an announced plan.
- It did not report a national percentage of wells exceeding 1 mg/L iron.

Retain the valid research as additions and qualifications, not as corrections to those nonexistent claims. A [USGS source](https://water.usgs.gov/nwc/NWC/water_quality/tables/quality.abs.html) does support conductivity-based dissolved-solids estimation and its composition dependence, so the supplied audit's inability to retrieve it does not invalidate that citation.

## Remaining scope limits

“EDTA is in most Dove shampoos,” “the circular was most likely issued in July and recirculated in August,” and “no local brand sells hard-water shampoo” need narrower wording or additional evidence. Two examples, conflicting report dates and unsuccessful searches do not establish those general conclusions.

The supplied audit does not examine Pani Map's household-level promise, prototype performance, supplier cost/capacity, the submission email or the financial calculations. It therefore cannot replace the earlier full-file audit. Its absence of a financial objection does not validate the model; the terminal working-capital disclosure and combined-versus-volume-only downside findings remain relevant.

**Use the verified additions, correct the overstatements, and keep the original concept's proposed benefits and unverified rules clearly labelled.**
